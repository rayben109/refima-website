Company Overview & Profile

The Molecular Backbone of Tanzanian Mining

REFIMA Company Limited is a specialized chemical supply enterprise dedicated to supporting the mining and industrial sectors across Tanzania — fully authorized by the Government Chemist Laboratory Authority (GCLA) and the Mining Commission.

Corporate Background

Authorized. Audited. Delivering purity at scale. REFIMA Company Limited provides high-purity gold extraction chemicals, industrial acids, flotation reagents, and laboratory compounds.
