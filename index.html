About Refima

A Dynamic Engineering & Supply Partner, Built for Tanzania

Founded in 2021, REFIMA Company Limited is a private limited company committed to excellence in engineering, logistics, and construction supply. With a growing reputation for reliability and innovation, we serve local and international clients with precision and care — from mine sites in Kahama to ports in Dar es Salaam.
