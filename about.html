About Refima

We Are REFIMA Company Limited

Founded in 2021, REFIMA is a dynamic private limited company committed to excellence in engineering, logistics, and construction supply — serving local and international clients with precision and care.

2021Company Founded
Our Story

Precision, Integrity & Growth Since 2021

REFIMA Company Limited was founded to close the gap between Tanzania's ambitious infrastructure and mining growth, and the reliable supply chains needed to sustain it. What began as a spare-parts and construction-materials supplier has grown into a five-division engineering and industrial supply group — now including a GCLA-licensed mining chemicals and reagents business.

Today, we're proud to hold national-scale supply contracts with organizations including the Tanzania Ports Authority, TANESCO, Puma Energy Tanzania, and the Tanzania Wildlife Protection Fund — a track record built on reliability, technical expertise, and uncompromising quality.

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted partner in engineering, construction, mining supply and automotive solutions across Africa — inspiring innovation and driving development in every project we touch.

Our Mission

To create global career opportunities while delivering the best work environment for talent to thrive, and to supply our clients with reliable, high-quality, budget-conscious engineering and industrial solutions.
