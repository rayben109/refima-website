Our Services

Engineering & Industrial Supply, Built Around Your Site

Working with REFIMA means partnering with a team that prioritizes quality, efficiency, and long-term success — across mining, marine, automotive, and construction.

01  ·  Mining Division

Mining Equipment, Parts & Services

Heavy-duty machinery, critical spare parts, and field services for mining sites — ensuring safe, efficient, and uninterrupted mineral extraction operations. Paired with our GCLA-licensed chemicals division, REFIMA is a true end-to-end mining supply partner.

  • Heavy machinery & wear-part sourcing
  • On-site field service & maintenance support
  • Direct HAZMAT & equipment logistics to remote mine sites
View Mining Chemicals Portfolio
02  ·  Marine Division

Marine Spare Parts

REFIMA provides top-quality marine spare parts to support the needs of shipping companies, offshore operators, and marine service providers. Our offerings include components for engines, navigation, deck equipment, and safety systems.

  • Marine engine & propulsion components
  • Navigation, deck equipment & safety systems
  • Fast sourcing, durable and industry-compliant parts
03  ·  Automotive Division

Motor Vehicle Spare Parts

REFIMA is your reliable source for motor vehicle spare parts, serving both private and commercial fleets. We stock a wide range of components for cars, trucks, buses, and trailers.

  • Engine, suspension & electrical systems
  • Braking systems & performance-tested components
  • Consistent supply for garages, mechanics & fleet operators
04  ·  Construction Division

Building Materials

REFIMA delivers a comprehensive range of construction and building materials tailored to the needs of developers, contractors, and retailers — premium cement, tiles, paints, plumbing fixtures, adhesives, and structural materials sourced from trusted manufacturers.

  • Cement, tiles & structural materials
  • Paint, plumbing supplies & adhesives
  • Engineered for homes, offices & infrastructure
